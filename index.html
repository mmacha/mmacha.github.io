Meghanath Macha

I build models from where people go and what they do online.

At ZeroToOne, I lead AI and platform. We turn where people go and what they do online into predictions of visits and intent, and get them into the platforms where they are used. I started out building the models and still design the systems. Much of my work now is making the data, products and teams around them work.

Head of AI & Platform, ZeroToOne

About

At ZeroToOne, we're replacing separate audience models with one model trained on physical and digital behavior. It scores 1.7B+ IDs a day across 9,000+ behavioral categories, and the audiences built on it refresh daily. We also built Audience Builder, which lets people describe an audience in plain English and check what the system actually built.

I led the technical diligence for GroundTruth and WeatherBug and now lead bringing the platforms and teams together. Before that, I was a founding engineer on Adobe GenStudio, working on how generative AI could follow a company's brand guidelines.

I have a PhD from Carnegie Mellon, where I studied privacy and location data. Nine granted patents. Papers in ISR, KDD, DMKD and Management Science.

I live in California with my wife, daughter and our cat, Binxy.

Selected work

01

One model, many audiences

We used to train a separate model for each audience. Now one behavioral model produces predictions across thousands of categories, and the audiences built from it are refreshed every day and delivered inside the ad platform.

02

Getting AI to follow brand rules

At Adobe GenStudio, I worked on grounding content generation in a knowledge graph of a company's brand guidelines. The rules could be checked independently of the model making the content.

03

Building audiences from plain English

Audience requests used to go through engineers. Now a planner describes who they want to reach in plain English, and the agent builds the audience from our predictions. The definition stays visible, so people can check what the system understood before they use it.
