Meghanath Macha

I architect behavioral foundation models and the platforms that run them in production.

At ZeroToOne.AI, I lead AI and platform. We train a spatiotemporal model on where people go and what they do online to predict visits and intent. We score about 3 billion device IDs a day. I design the inference, identity, and data systems that serve those predictions and lead the teams integrating GroundTruth and WeatherBug.

Head of AI & Platform, ZeroToOne.AI

Selected work

01

One model, many products

We replaced separate audience classifiers with a shared model that powers daily predictive audiences and provides a starting point for warehouse enrichment and models for churn, customer value, and next best action. If a retailer’s campaign rewards cheap clicks, training on those clicks would teach the model to predict clicks instead of store visits. We keep bidding separate so a visit score means the same thing across campaigns. In March 2026, Horizon Media reported 48% greater visitation efficiency versus baseline in one pilot combining our predictions with its own audience intelligence and campaign design.

We generate embeddings on Vertex AI GPUs and score audiences on CPUs, so scoring can run without waiting for GPU capacity once the embeddings are ready. Before releasing a model update, we check its predictions across audience categories and time windows. Read more about how we built the ML platform.

02

Getting AI to follow brand rules

A draft can sound right and still miss a required trademark symbol. At Adobe GenStudio, I built the verification layer. We represented brand guidelines in a knowledge graph and checked drafts separately from generation. In GenStudio, reviewers could see which rule a draft failed, revise the asset, and rerun the check before sending it for approval. The verification layer shipped in October 2024 with GenStudio for Performance Marketing.

03

Audiences you can describe and check

When our audience requests went through engineers, it took about two weeks to get a usable audience. With Audience Builder, a planner describes who they want to reach and the agent writes a definition over the model’s predictions. An answer can sound reasonable while dropping a constraint like geography. Planners can read and correct the filters before activation and reuse the definition. In GroundTruth Ads Manager, custom audiences are available within 24 hours.

04

Deciding what to buy and what to build

I led technical diligence on what to integrate, what to rebuild, and where the data and identity systems overlapped before ZeroToOne.AI acquired GroundTruth and WeatherBug. I now lead the integration, which has to keep two live ad platforms running. We moved legacy custom audiences to Audience Builder and consolidated GroundTruth’s direct audience delivery, cutting redundant compute while keeping existing audience IDs stable for live campaigns. In GroundTruth Ads Manager, we grew the predictive catalog from about 60 to more than 1,500 segments and made them available alongside standard visitation audiences so advertisers could compare them in the same campaign workflow.

About

I’ve taken models from research into production at the Carnegie Mellon spin-out that became ZeroToOne.AI, as a founding engineer on Adobe GenStudio, and again after returning to ZeroToOne.AI to build the Large Behavioral Model. I have a PhD in Information Systems from Carnegie Mellon, where I worked on privacy-preserving models of location data. I’ve published in ISR, KDD, DMKD, and Management Science.

I design systems so people can inspect what they’re about to use. That means keeping audience filters readable, checking brand rules separately from generation, and keeping the meaning of an intent score consistent across products.
